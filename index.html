How to use KAI
Your intelligent assistant — quick reference
📅 Create calendar events
"Remind me on Friday to complete H&S training"
"Remind me in 2 days to follow up with Marcus"
"Block Monday morning for deep work"
"Add budget review to my calendar on Thursday at 3pm"
"Create a meeting with Priya on Wednesday at 10am"
"Look for some time on Friday for the strategy paper"
📝 Save actions and reminders
"Remember I need to send the strategy paper by Friday"
"Remind me to chase James about the Control Plane by Monday"
"Note down that I agreed to present at Tuesday's board"
→ Saves to Open Actions in your briefing, synced to Drive
💬 Ask KAI anything
"What should I prioritise today?"
"Show me emails from Sarah Chen"
"Which emails haven't I replied to?"
"How does Wednesday look?"
"Draft a reply to Marcus about the governance board"
"What are my open actions?"
"Any urgent emails I've missed?"
